The idea

Why two wallets?

Korean transit gates read a transit balance. Shops read a payment balance. They are genuinely separate systems, which is why most visitors end up carrying two cards. This one holds both, and you move money between them at any kiosk.

Prepaid wallet

The main one — pays like a card

Shops, restaurants, convenience stores and other affiliated merchants.

Transit wallet

Taps you through gates

Bus, subway, and convenience stores that accept transit cards.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need a Korean bank account or phone number?
No. The card carries no name and needs no registration. At a kiosk you can buy it and top it up without a Korean bank account, phone number, or ID.
Do I need ID?
No. It is a bearer card with no name on it, and no ID is needed to top up.
What if I load more than the limit?
Each wallet holds up to KRW 500,000. Anything above that limit is handed back to you in KRW cash on the spot.
